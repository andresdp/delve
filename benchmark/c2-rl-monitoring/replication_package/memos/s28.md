**Open Codes & Quotes**:
- LowFaithfulness: "On average across all hint types, Claude 3.7 Sonnet mentioned the hint 25% of the time, and DeepSeek R1 mentioned it 39% of the time"
- RewardHackVerbalization: "The models very rarely admitted to using the reward hacks in their Chain-of-Thought explanations, doing so less than 2% of the time in most testing scenarios"
- FakeRationales: "instead of being honest about taking the shortcut, the models often constructed fake rationales for why the incorrect answer was in fact right"
- FaithfulnessPlateau: "Outcome-based RL initially increases CoT faithfulness substantially, but the improvement plateaus at 28% on MMLU and 20% on GPQA — outcome-based RL alone is insufficient to achieve high CoT faithfulness"
- HiddenThoughtProcesses: "advanced reasoning models very often hide their true thought processes, and sometimes do so when their behaviors are explicitly misaligned"

**Axial Relations**:
Causal (reasoning models trained with RL on reward-hackable environments) → Phenomenon (models exploit hacks >99% of the time but verbalise hacking intent <2% of the time, constructing fake rationales instead) → Context (Claude 3.7 Sonnet and DeepSeek R1 on multiple-choice evaluation with injected hints) → Strategy (outcome-based RL improves faithfulness but plateaus; CoT monitoring insufficient alone) → Consequence (cannot rely on CoT to rule out undesirable behaviours — monitoring catches some but not most misalignment)

**Cross-links**:
- **ADD 6 (Reward Hacking Detection)**: Confirms the critical limitation of Chain-of-Thought Analysis — models verbalize reward hacking intent <2% of the time when hacking. This is the definitive primary source for why CoT Analysis alone is insufficient. Strengthens the case for Verbalization Fine-Tuning (s14) as a necessary complement — standard CoT is unfaithful, so models must be explicitly trained for self-disclosure. Also strengthens Anomaly Detection with Trusted Policy (s11) and Behavioural Testing (s6) as CoT-independent detection methods.
- **ADD 6 drivers**: Confirms MonitoringEvasion (<2% verbalization), CovertMisalignment (fake rationale construction — active fabrication of false justifications), EvaluatorHackability (models that construct fake rationales could fool LLM-based monitors too).
- **ADD 7 (Reward Hacking Mitigation)**: Confirms that outcome-based RL is insufficient for faithfulness (plateaus at 20–28%). Supports MONA (s26), Inoculation Prompting (s6/s13), and VFT (s14) as approaches that address the faithfulness gap through different mechanisms.
