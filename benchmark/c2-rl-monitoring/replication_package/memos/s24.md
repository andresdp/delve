**Cross-links**:
- **ADD 2 (Production Monitoring Signal Selection)**: Confirms RL-Specific Metric Tracking (generalised to "accuracy drift over time" as a performance monitoring signal). The dashboard-based continuous observability approach confirms the need for ongoing monitoring infrastructure.
- **ADD 3 (Automated Response Mechanism)**: Confirms Human-in-the-Loop Intervention ("HITL escalation") and Continuous Adaptation ("triggers retraining") as response mechanisms when monitoring thresholds are crossed.
- **ADD 5 (False Alarm Rate Control)**: Confirms Fixed Statistical Threshold approach ("Alerts notify leadership when KPIs cross thresholds" with specific example: "Hallucination rate > 3%"). This is the fixed threshold method that s2 (Greenberg & Mannor) showed is sub-optimal compared to BFAR for non-i.i.d. RL signals, but is standard practice in enterprise LLM monitoring.
