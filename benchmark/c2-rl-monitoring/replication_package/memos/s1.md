## Memo 1: Production RL Monitoring and Safety Architecture (Source 1)

**URL**: https://www.runpod.io/articles/guides/reinforcement-learning-in-production-building-adaptive-ai-systems-that-learn-from-experience

**Open Codes & Quotes**:
- EnvironmentNonStationarity: "Production environments are non-stationary, partially observable, and contain uncertainties that laboratory simulations cannot fully capture"
- DistributionShiftHandling: "RL agents trained in simulation or controlled environments must adapt to production distribution shifts while maintaining safe and effective behavior"
- SafeExploration: "Production RL requires safe exploration techniques that prevent agents from taking actions that could harm users, damage systems, or violate business constraints"
- ConservativeUpdates: "Implement conservative policy update mechanisms that ensure new policies don't deviate dramatically from proven baseline behaviors, reducing the risk of catastrophic performance degradation"
- ConstrainedOptimization: "Deploy constrained optimization techniques that incorporate safety constraints directly into the learning process"
- CircuitBreakers: "Deploy automated circuit breaker systems that detect poor performance and switch to safe fallback policies before significant damage occurs"
- RealTimeMonitoring: "Implement comprehensive monitoring that tracks RL agent performance, reward signals, exploration patterns, and system health in real-time to detect issues quickly"
- HITLOversight: "Design human oversight mechanisms that enable domain experts to monitor RL behavior, provide feedback, and intervene when necessary"
- RollbackRecovery: "Production RL systems need robust mechanisms for detecting poor performance and rolling back to safe baseline policies when learning goes wrong"
- OfflineRL: "Implement offline RL techniques that learn from historical data without requiring direct environment interaction, reducing risks associated with online exploration"
- MetaLearning: "Deploy meta-learning approaches that enable RL agents to quickly adapt to new tasks or environment changes with minimal additional training"
- RLSpecificMetrics: "Implement monitoring systems that track RL-specific metrics including reward signals, exploration rates, policy entropy, and learning progress"
- BusinessImpact: "Deploy measurement systems that correlate RL agent behavior with business outcomes, enabling continuous optimization of reward functions"
- ABTesting: "Implement A/B testing frameworks that compare RL policies against baseline approaches and previous policy versions to measure improvement and detect regressions"
- EnvironmentChangeDetection: "Implement continual learning techniques, environment change detection, policy adaptation mechanisms, and robust baseline fallbacks"
- FailSafe: "Implement fail-safe mechanisms that ensure RL systems degrade gracefully when encountering unexpected situations"
- BiasDetection: "Deploy bias detection systems that identify and mitigate unfair or discriminatory behaviors that may emerge from RL policy learning"
- MultiAgent: "Production RL often involves multiple learning agents, human users, and external systems creating complex interaction dynamics that can lead to emergent behaviors and potential instabilities"

**Axial Relations**:
Causal (non-stationary production environments + distribution shifts + multi-agent interactions) → Phenomenon (deployed RL agent performance degradation, unsafe exploration, emergent instabilities) → Context (business-critical production systems requiring continuous adaptation) → Strategy (conservative policy updates, constrained optimization, circuit breakers, real-time monitoring, HITL oversight, rollback mechanisms) → Consequence (safe adaptive system with 25-60% metric improvements, or catastrophic failure and safety violations)

**Dimensions**:
- ExplorationSafety: unconstrained exploration (research setting) vs constrained safe exploration (production)
- LearningMode: online RL (adaptive but risky) vs offline RL (safe but static) vs hybrid
- MonitoringGranularity: RL-specific metrics (reward signals, policy entropy, exploration rates) vs business outcome metrics (user engagement, revenue)
- OversightLevel: fully automated (circuit breakers) vs human-in-the-loop vs full human control
- PolicyUpdateStrategy: conservative (small deviations from baseline) vs aggressive (fast adaptation)
- ResponseMechanism: rollback to baseline vs graceful degradation vs continued operation with constraints

**Forces/Drivers**:
- "EnvironmentNonStationarity": production environments change continuously, requiring agents that detect and adapt to shifts
- "SafetyConstraints": business and regulatory requirements mandate that agents cannot take harmful actions during exploration
- "DistributionShift": gap between training/simulation and production distributions degrades policy performance
- "EmergentInstability": multi-agent interactions in production create unpredictable dynamics
- "DelayedSparseRewards": real-world reward signals are noisy, delayed, and sparse, complicating performance monitoring
- "CatastrophicDegradation": aggressive policy updates risk sudden, severe performance drops

**Cross-links**: First source with ADDs — establishes core monitoring architecture for deployed RL systems.

## ADD 2: Production Monitoring Signal Selection (Source 1 Primary)

**Description**: Decide what signals to monitor for a deployed RL agent to detect performance degradation, unsafe behaviour, or environmental change. The choice determines what types of problems can be detected and how quickly. RL-specific metrics (reward signals, exploration rates, policy entropy) catch RL-internal issues; business metrics catch outcome-level problems; A/B comparison catches relative regressions.

| Decision Options | Source Quote | Memo Link |
|---|---|---|
| RL-Specific Metric Tracking | "track RL-specific metrics including reward signals, exploration rates, policy entropy, and learning progress" | Memo 1 |
| Business Outcome Correlation | "correlate RL agent behavior with business outcomes, enabling continuous optimization of reward functions" | Memo 1 |
| A/B Policy Comparison | "A/B testing frameworks that compare RL policies against baseline approaches and previous policy versions to measure improvement and detect regressions" | Memo 1 |
| Covariance-Weighted Reward Signal | "an optimal test using weighted mean of rewards with inverse covariance row-sums" — formally grounded reward monitoring | Memo 2 (S2) |
| Feature Distribution Drift (KL/MMD) | "Drift index: Statistical distance between dataset and live — KL or MMD on features" — monitors input distribution shifts | Memo 4 (S4) |
| Reward Proxy Gap Tracking | "Reward proxy gap: Difference between offline reward and live proxy" — monitors eval-to-production divergence | Memo 4 (S4) |
| Embedding Drift Detection | "embedding-based drift detection — analyzing embedding clusters over time" — detects semantic shift in states/inputs | Memo 8 (S8) |
| Activation Delta Monitoring | "model's own internal activation patterns can reveal task drift" — internal model signal for drift | Memo 8 (S8) |
| Reward Model Score Monitoring | "score responses using the frozen reward model from the RLHF training phase" — monitors RM predictions on live traffic | Memo 9 (S9) |
| KL Divergence from Reference Policy | "track KL divergence between deployed policy and original SFT policy" — structural measure of policy drift | Memo 9 (S9) |
| Metric Divergence Monitoring | "If optimized metric improves while related metrics stagnate/decline, suspect gaming" — cross-metric comparison | Memo 6 (S6) |
| Shield Intervention Rate Tracking | "Intervention rate under shift is one of the first signals that your safety envelope has stopped matching reality" — monitor how often safety shield blocks actions as drift proxy | Memo 16 (S16) |
| Context-Sliced Signal Monitoring | "compute diagnostics sliced by interaction mode (pre-tool vs post-tool) — catch instabilities before they turn into divergence" — monitor per context type, not just aggregate | Memo 18 (S18) |

| Decision Drivers (Forces) | Impact | Source Quote |
|---|---|---|
| EnvironmentNonStationarity | +RLSpecific (detects internal policy issues) | "non-stationary, partially observable" |
| DelayedSparseRewards | +BusinessOutcome (captures delayed effects) | "delayed, sparse, or noisy" |
| CatastrophicDegradation | +ABComparison (catches regressions against baseline) | "detect regressions" |
| MonitoringIndependence | +All external monitoring options | "entirely external and independent of the agent" — Memo 2 (S2) |
| SignalNonStationarity | +CovarianceWeighted, -NaiveMean | "rewards within episodes are correlated and non-i.i.d." — Memo 2 (S2) |
| GoodhartsLaw | Negative for any single-metric approach | "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure" — Memo 5 (S5) |
| CovariateShift | +FeatureDistributionDrift, +EmbeddingDrift | "training data may systematically differ from production" — Memo 4 (S4) |
| EvaluationMismatch | +RewardProxyGap | "offline eval metrics may correlate poorly with live performance" — Memo 4 (S4) |
| RewardModelStaleness | +KLDivergence (structural, RM-independent) | "frozen reward model may become invalid proxy over time" — Memo 9 (S9) |
| AlignmentImpermanence | +RewardModelScoring, +KLDivergence | "alignment is not a one-time achievement" — Memo 9 (S9) |
| ScaleCompounding | +Multi-signal approaches | "more capable agents find more sophisticated hacks" — single-metric insufficient at scale — Memo 6 (S6) |
| HiddenVariableShift | +ShieldInterventionRate, +EmbeddingDrift, Negative for state-based-only monitoring | "hidden parameters change true risk of same visible state-action pair — nothing looks wrong until action lands" — Memo 16 (S16) |

## ADD 3: Automated Response Mechanism Selection (Source 1 Primary)

**Description**: Choose how the system responds when monitoring detects that a deployed RL agent is performing poorly or behaving unsafely. Circuit breakers provide fast automated response; rollback reverts to known-good policy; graceful degradation limits agent capability while maintaining service. The decision trades off response speed against operational disruption.

| Decision Options | Source Quote | Memo Link |
|---|---|---|
| Automated Circuit Breakers | "detect poor performance and switch to safe fallback policies before significant damage occurs" | Memo 1 |
| Policy Rollback | "robust mechanisms for detecting poor performance and rolling back to safe baseline policies" | Memo 1 |
| Rollback with Stabilisation | "reload checkpoint and resume training with smaller learning rate" — rollback alone insufficient without dampening | Memo 3 (S3) |
| Blue-Green Traffic Shift | "rapidly shifting 100% of traffic back to previous stable version — managed at load balancer or service mesh" | Memo 7 (S7) |
| Graceful Degradation | "fail-safe mechanisms that ensure RL systems degrade gracefully when encountering unexpected situations" | Memo 1 |
| Human-in-the-Loop Intervention | "human oversight mechanisms that enable domain experts to monitor RL behavior, provide feedback, and intervene when necessary" | Memo 1 |
| Continuous Adaptation (Fine-tuning) | Retraining trigger taxonomy: time-based vs threshold-based vs event-based fine-tuning as response to drift | Memo 8 (S8) |
| Mid-Run Reward Function Update | "Updating reward functions mid-run" and "adding constraints" — modifies agent's objective rather than rolling back | Memo 6 (S6) |

| Decision Drivers (Forces) | Impact | Source Quote |
|---|---|---|
| CatastrophicDegradation | +CircuitBreakers (fastest response) | "before significant damage occurs" |
| SafetyConstraints | +HITL for high-stakes domains | "domain experts to monitor RL behavior and intervene" |
| EnvironmentNonStationarity | +GracefulDegradation (maintain partial service) | "degrade gracefully when encountering unexpected situations" |
| EmergentInstability | +Rollback (revert to known state) | "rolling back to safe baseline policies when learning goes wrong" |
| StatefulRollbackComplexity | +BlueGreen (infrastructure-level, avoids state issues), -SimpleRollback for RL | "RL agents carry learned state requiring state restoration/reset" — Memo 7 (S7) |
| FlappingRisk | -Aggressive triggers for all automated options | "setting thresholds too aggressively leads to constant rollback/redeploy" — Memo 7 (S7) |
| AuditRequirement | +All options (must log comprehensively) | "every rollback event must be logged — trigger, timestamp, versions" — Memo 7 (S7) |
| RetrainingCost | +ContinuousAdaptation when affordable, +Rollback when not | "full retraining prohibitively expensive" — Memo 8 (S8) |
| CatastrophicForgetting | -ContinuousAdaptation (risk losing old capabilities) | "updating on new data risks forgetting" — Memo 8 (S8) |
