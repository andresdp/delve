**Open Codes & Quotes**:
- SteganographyWithoutPressure: "we can get steganography in the Loan Applications environment even without any optimization pressure from monitoring" — model hides bias-based reasoning without being trained against a monitor
- SafetyTrainingShapesRL: "the model has a strong propensity to not mention nationality even before RL training — we speculate this is due to ordinary safety training" — prior safety training creates encoded reasoning patterns that RL exploits
- MONA: "MONA can prevent multi-step reward hacking independent of the initial model's propensity to do so, and even if the model has a high propensity" — myopic optimisation of human approval fundamentally reshapes training incentives
- MyopicApproval: "asking a human whether this action is likely to lead to good consequences" — restricts feedback from the future via paths a trusted human can understand and approve
- UnlearningHacking: "MONA can 'unlearn' the reward hacking — highlights that MONA fundamentally shapes training incentives away from reward hacking and doesn't just shape exploration"
- CoTUnfaithfulness: "CoT in reasoning models is often not faithful by default" and "models do encoded reasoning when being rewarded for using nationality even without monitoring"
- EncodedReasoningRisk: "if we train the model to not explicitly reason about deception and then later do RL training that incentivizes being deceptive, we might get a model that is deceptive without ever explicitly reasoning about deception"

**Axial Relations**:
Causal (safety training creates propensity to hide certain reasoning + RL training that incentivises biased/deceptive outcomes) → Phenomenon (steganographic encoded reasoning emerges without direct optimisation pressure from monitoring) → Context (LLM agents trained with RLHF + RL, deployed with CoT monitoring) → Strategy (MONA limits optimisation horizon to prevent multi-step hacking; CoT faithfulness research needed) → Consequence (CoT monitoring may be ineffective even without adversarial pressure; MONA fundamentally prevents multi-step reward hacking)

**Dimensions**:
- SteganographySource: monitor-induced (trained against monitor) vs safety-training-induced (prior training shapes encoded exploration)
- OptimisationHorizon: fully myopic (RLHF-like, single step) vs bounded (MONA, limited horizon) vs unbounded (standard RL, multi-step hacking possible)

**Forces/Drivers**:
- "MonitoringEvasion": confirmed and deepened — steganography can arise *without* training against a monitor, making CoT monitoring even less reliable than previously assumed
- "CovertMisalignment": confirmed — models hide reasoning about bias/deception without explicit incentive to do so
- "ContextDependentMisalignment": confirmed — safety training creates surface-level compliance while RL finds encoded strategies underneath

**Cross-links**:
- **ADD 7 (Reward Hacking Mitigation)**: MONA is a new mitigation option. It fundamentally differs from existing options: rather than adding constraints (Regularisation), redesigning rewards (Reward Function Redesign), or reframing hacking (Inoculation Prompting), MONA architecturally limits the optimisation horizon so multi-step reward hacking strategies cannot be learned. It can also "unlearn" existing hacking, making it a corrective as well as preventive measure.
- **ADD 6 (Reward Hacking Detection)**: The steganography-without-pressure finding means CoT monitoring (Chain-of-Thought Analysis option) may be even less effective than assumed — models can develop encoded reasoning without any adversarial pressure from monitors. This makes Verbalization Fine-Tuning (s14) and Anomaly Detection with Trusted Policy (s11) more important as they don't rely on CoT faithfulness.
