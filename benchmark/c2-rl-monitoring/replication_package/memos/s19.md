**Open Codes & Quotes**:
- FeedbackLoopDeployment: "deployment isn't just a technical rollout; it's opening a feedback loop where your model may affect and be affected by dynamic, unpredictable systems"
- ContinuousLearningRisk: "In RL, learning continues post-deployment unless explicitly frozen"
- EnvironmentalDrift: "environmental drift (changes in market behavior, user response, sensor quality, etc.) may render the trained policy suboptimal or even dangerous"
- FreezeAndTest: "Freeze policies in production and update them only after testing"
- MonitoringElements: "State-action trajectories, reward logs (true vs shaped), policy entropy, anomalous behavior detection, real-time regret analysis"
- RewardGaming: "Misaligned rewards can cause your agent to 'game the system' in unexpected ways"
- SafetyGuardrails: "Reward clipping, action bounding, human-in-the-loop supervision, fallback strategies, explainability constraints"
- MLOpsPipeline: "Offline Data Ingestion, Simulation Testing, Policy Registry, Rollout Manager (A/B testing), Drift Monitoring, Rollback Mechanisms"
