**Open Codes & Quotes**:
- AggregateMetricBlindness: "metrics we normally monitor (loss, reward, mean KL) are aggregates that can remain stable while rare-but-catastrophic behavior grows in the tail"
- SliceAwareMonitoring: "compute diagnostics in-stream, slice them by interaction mode (pre-tool vs post-tool), and aggregate them across workers so we can catch instabilities before they turn into divergence"
- TailGrowth: "variance amplification can localize to tool-conditioned contexts and manifest as gradual tail growth — while aggregate metrics such as loss, reward, entropy, and global KL remain stable"
- ToolConditionedShift: "tool calls expand the reachable state space through external transitions — post-tool contexts often lie in regions where the reference policy assigns substantially lower probability mass"
- DelayedFailure: "By the time global metrics begin to shift, variance has already compounded substantially — late-phase instability is often misattributed to optimizer instability or insufficient global variance control"
- DistributionalNotThreshold: "The key signal is not a single threshold crossing, but a shape change in the right tail — probability mass migrates toward higher-magnitude updates"

**Axial Relations**:
Causal (tool-using agents encounter tool-conditioned states in low-support regions) → Phenomenon (variance amplification localised to tool contexts, invisible to aggregate metrics) → Context (production-scale on-policy RL training with long horizons and tool augmentation) → Strategy (context-sliced monitoring by interaction mode, tail percentile tracking, ESS as supporting signal) → Consequence (early detection of instability vs late discovery after compounding)

**Dimensions**:
- MonitoringGranularity: aggregate (mean reward, global KL — misses localised failures) vs context-sliced (pre-tool vs post-tool — catches localised instability)
- FailureSignal: threshold crossing (binary) vs distributional shape change (tail growth — more informative)
- FailureTiming: early (detectable with sliced diagnostics) vs late (only visible in aggregate after compounding)

**Forces/Drivers**:
- "DetectionLatency": confirmed — "by the time global metrics begin to shift, variance has already compounded substantially"
- "HiddenVariableShift": confirmed — tool-conditioned contexts create hidden distribution shift within the agent's own operation
- "ContextDependentMisalignment": confirmed — failures localise to specific interaction contexts while aggregate metrics look stable

**Cross-links**:
- **ADD 2 (Production Monitoring Signal Selection)**: The context-sliced monitoring approach is transferable to deployment: don't just track mean reward across all interactions — slice by context type (tool-using vs text-only, high-risk vs routine, novel vs familiar). If degradation is localised to specific interaction modes, aggregate monitoring will miss it entirely. This generalises to deployment monitoring as "Context-Sliced Signal Monitoring."
- **ADD 4 (Reward Degradation Test Statistic)**: The tail growth concept (tracking 95th percentile of deviation rather than mean) suggests that reward monitoring should track distributional properties, not just means. This is consistent with s2's covariance-weighted approach but adds the insight that tail behaviour is more informative than central tendency.
- **ADD 5 (False Alarm Rate Control)**: "The key signal is not a single threshold crossing, but a shape change in the right tail" — confirms that fixed thresholds are insufficient; monitoring must detect distributional changes.

### Notes

Although this source is primarily about training diagnostics, the core insight — **aggregate metrics can remain stable while dangerous behaviour grows in specific context slices** — is directly transferable to deployment monitoring. A deployed tool-using agent may show stable aggregate reward while degrading specifically in tool-conditioned interactions. Context-sliced monitoring is the remedy.

The Post-Training Toolkit (open-source) implements these diagnostics for training. An analogous deployment monitoring toolkit would slice production metrics by interaction context (tool type, risk level, novelty) rather than reporting only aggregate performance.
