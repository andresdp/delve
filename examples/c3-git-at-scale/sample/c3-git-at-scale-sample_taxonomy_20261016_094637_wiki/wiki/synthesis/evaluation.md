## What each criterion checks

- **Orthogonality** (threshold 0.5): Every dimension is a distinct decision point: no two dimensions ask the same question, and no dimension's candidate decisions are really options of another dimension.
- **Clarity** (threshold 0.5): Dimension names and descriptions make the decision question clear, and value labels and descriptions make the alternatives distinguishable, so a reader could tell which dimension and value a passage about a design decision belongs to.
- **Completeness** (threshold 0.5): The taxonomy contains the major decision points implied by the use case, each with its main candidate decisions.
- **Use case alignment** (threshold 0.5): Every dimension serves the stated use case; none is out of its scope.
- **No catch-alls** (threshold 0.5): No dimension or value is a vague catch-all ('Other', 'Miscellaneous', 'General', 'Other approaches').
- **Axis vs. value** (threshold 0.5): Each dimension is a decision point with alternatives, not a single option presented as a dimension; each value is an option, not a sub-topic.
- **One decision point** (threshold 0.5): Each dimension is a single decision point: all its candidate decisions are alternative answers to one question, rather than a broad topic bundling several questions. Where the use case names quality attributes or forces, dimensions whose choice is driven by recognizable quality attributes or trade-offs are preferred (quality-attribute grounding).
- **Rejected-alternative handling** (threshold 0.5): For each candidate decision, the taxonomy makes clear whether sources adopted it, rejected it, or disagree (status 'accepted', 'rejected' or 'mixed'); a rejected alternative is never presented as adopted; each candidate decision appears once with its stance; outcomes (status 'outcome') are kept apart from candidate decisions.
- **Dimensional coverage** (threshold 0.5): Every sampled passage that discusses a design decision can be placed under at least one decision point (dimension) of the taxonomy.
- **Candidate-decision coverage** (threshold 0.5): The options that the sampled passages adopt or reject are named as values (candidate decisions) of the right dimension.
- **Value exclusivity** (threshold 0.5): Each system (source) adopts one value per dimension: the values of a dimension are alternatives, not features used together. Deterministic: computed from the evidence, without a judge.
