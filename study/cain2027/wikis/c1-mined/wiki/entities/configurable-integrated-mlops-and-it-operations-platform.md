## Includes

This alternative was defined from values the sources combine (value recoding); they are its facets:

- **MLOps lifecycle automation**: Automates infrastructure, deployment, and operational workflow steps across the ML lifecycle.
- **Configurable MLOps practices**: Adapting the breadth and configuration of MLOps practices to team and organizational circumstances.
- **Out-of-the-box MLOps solution**: A pre-built MLOps platform provides lifecycle infrastructure without assembling a dedicated internal operations capability.
- **Composable MLOps toolchain**: Combines replaceable specialized services and custom integrations into an end-to-end MLOps system.
- **MLOps investment level**: Selects the degree of lifecycle automation and integration according to organizational size, model count, and update frequency.
- **DataOps-only ML lifecycle management**: Uses DataOps without model-lifecycle capabilities to manage an ML project.
- **AIOps-driven IT operations automation**: Uses big data and ML to detect and proactively resolve day-to-day IT operations issues.
